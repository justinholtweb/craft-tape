Consent Mode v2, on by default

Unknown is
not denied

Consent has three states. "Not yet answered" is its own, and it decides whether a tag waits or gives up.

Reads Cookiebot, CookieYes, Complianz, Iubenda, OneTrust, Osano, Termly, Klaro, Usercentrics and Civic, or a JavaScript expression of your own. With your own banner, it is one call: tape.consent.update()

GrantedThe tag loadsIts pixel loads and its events fire. Server-side sends for it go ahead.
Not yet answeredThe tag waitsEvents are held in the page, not dropped. Accepting the banner starts tracking on this page, not the next one.
DeniedThe tag gives upOther pixels never load and Google's run cookieless, as Consent Mode intends. Server-side sends are skipped, and recovery leaves that order alone.
